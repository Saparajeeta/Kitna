# Kitna demo

Indian agents can misread "dhai lakh", "parso", "saade teen baje". Kitna reads them exactly, or
tells you it's unsure.

**Pre-alpha.** The rules were drafted by an AI and have not been reviewed by native Hindi speakers.
No accuracy has been measured. Do not rely on it for real payments, bookings or records.

- Paste a message; every amount, date and time is highlighted with a plain-language reading.
- When a phrase has two meanings, you choose; nothing is guessed.
- The "Reviewer mode" page lets Hindi speakers mark draft cases ok / wrong / unnatural and download a CSV.
- Nothing you type is stored. Feedback leaves your device only if you send it yourself.

Source, limits and what has been measured: https://github.com/Saparajeeta/Kitna

# Ten worked examples

These show how a brief turns into a line. **The example lines were written by an AI, not by
a native speaker.** They may sound stiff. Do not copy their wording or spelling; write your
own. Today is Thursday 12 November 2026, 10:00 am in every brief.

| # | Brief | One possible line (AI-written) | What to notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask to send money now. Payee: Ramesh. Amount: ₹2,50,000. | Ramesh ko dhai lakh rupaye bhej do | "2.5 lakh", "2,50,000" or "dhai lakh": your choice. |
| 2 | Ask to send money on a later day. Payee: Sunita. Amount: ₹1,500. Day: Fri 13 Nov 2026. | Sunita ko kal pandrah sau rupaye bhejna hai | Friday the 13th is tomorrow, so "kal" is natural. "13 ko" is fine too. |
| 3 | Ask to pay a bill now. Bill: electricity. Amount: ₹3,750. | Bijli ka bill bhar do, paune chaar hazaar ka hai | Every fact is there; the order is free. |
| 4 | Ask to book a cab. From: Indiranagar. To: airport. Day: Fri 13 Nov 2026. Time: 5:30 AM. | Kal subah saade paanch baje Indiranagar se airport ke liye cab book karni hai | Morning must be clear here, because the brief says AM. |
| 5 | Ask for a cab from Whitefield to station tomorrow at 5 o'clock. Say only the number: no 'morning', 'evening', 'am' or 'pm'. | Kal 5 baje Whitefield se station ke liye cab chahiye | The brief wants the time left unclear. Do not add "subah" or "shaam". |
| 6 | Ask to send money to Kavita but do NOT say how much. | Kavita ko paise bhej do | Something is left out on purpose. |
| 7 | Ask for a reminder to take my medicine, 150 minutes from now. Say it as a length of time from now, not as a clock time. | Dhai ghante baad dawai lene ka reminder laga do | "2.5 ghante", "dhai ghante", "2 ghante 30 min": your choice. Not "12:30 baje". |
| 8 | Ask to add a calendar event: team meeting. Day: Mon 16 Nov 2026. Start: 3:00 PM. Length: 90 minutes. | Somvaar ko dopahar 3 baje dedh ghante ki team meeting rakh do | Three facts: day, start, length. |
| 9 | Ask a friend-style question: will you come to Goa with me? No booking wanted. (The assistant should not call any tool.) | Mere saath Goa chaloge? | Some lines ask for nothing. Just say them. |
| 10 | Report a problem with a delivery of order OD-51902. For the day, use only the word 'kal' and give no other hint about whether it already happened. | Order OD-51902 ki kal ki delivery ka issue hai | Keep "kal" unclear: no "tha", "hoga", "aayi", "aayegi". |

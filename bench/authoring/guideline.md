# Writing guideline (one page)

Thank you for helping. You will write short messages the way you would type them to an
assistant app, in **Hinglish with English letters**. It takes about 30 seconds per line.

## What you get

A spreadsheet with one row per situation:

| scenario_id | domain | brief | roman_utterance | writer_id | comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1-pay-003 | payments | Today is Thu 12 Nov 2026, 10:00 am. Ask to pay a bill now. Bill: electricity. Amount: ₹7,500. | *(you type here)* | w01 | |

## What to do

1. Read the brief. It says what you want and gives the facts.
2. In `roman_utterance`, type what **you** would actually send. One message, one or two sentences.
3. Leave the other columns alone. Use `comment` if a brief is confusing or unnatural.

## Rules

- **Your own style.** Spell words the way you normally do (`hazaar`, `hajar`, `hzr`, `2.5 lakh`,
  `dhai lakh`: all fine). Mix in English as you normally would. Do not make it "correct".
- **Say the facts your way.** The brief writes ₹2,50,000 and "Fri 13 Nov". You might type
  "dhai lakh", "2.5 lac", "kal", "13 ko". Use whatever you would really say, as long as it
  means the same thing on the day stated in the brief.
- **Include every fact** in the brief, and nothing extra.
- **Follow "do NOT say" instructions.** Some briefs ask you to leave something out, or to say
  only "5 baje" with no morning or evening word. That is on purpose.
- **English letters only.** No Devanagari. Digits are fine.
- **Nothing real.** No real phone numbers, UPI IDs, addresses or account numbers. Use only
  the names and order IDs in the brief.
- **No tools, no AI.** Do not use a translator, a chatbot or autocomplete suggestions, and do
  not look at how Kitna or anyone else wrote the line. If you have used the Kitna parser,
  please tell the project owner before writing.
- **Do not copy** the worked examples' wording. They only show the idea.

## Things that are fine

Short forms (`pls`, `kr do`, `bhej dena`), typos you would normally make, no punctuation,
starting with "bhai" or "please", putting the amount first or last.

## When a brief is wrong

If a brief is unclear, sounds like something nobody would ask, or you cannot say it without
adding or dropping a fact: leave the line empty and write why in `comment`. That is useful.

## Afterwards

Send the file back as CSV (UTF-8). Your lines will be published under CC BY 4.0 with an
anonymous writer id (`w01`), not your name, unless you ask to be credited.
